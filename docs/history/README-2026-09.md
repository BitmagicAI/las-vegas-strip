# Vegas: Afterlight
A new Bitmagic game project (ID `ZWLJZE4P1VA6`) with a Blender-authored, compressed central Las Vegas Strip environment.

## Open
Run `bitmagic dev` from this directory. The allocated editor URL is http://localhost:3091/ and game-only preview is http://localhost:3090/. Use the URL printed by the command if ports change.

Click Play. WASD moves; hold either Shift key to run at 10 m/s (normal movement stays 5 m/s); mouse looks; Space jumps; Escape pauses. The current preview is first-person while the requested Hero/WebHuman player is pending. Spawn is inside the cyan sanctuary. Walk within 3 m of each marked supply cache, then return inside the sanctuary to bank it. The counter completes at four caches. No combat or damage is enabled in this environment pass.

## Assets and authoring
- `build/level/vegas.blend`: editable source geometry, packed material maps, organized architecture collections.
- `build/bellagio-v2/bellagio-review.blend`: current level with presentation lighting and eight inspection cameras.
- `tools/level/bellagio.py`: curved Bellagio facade, cupola, lake, podium interior and room authoring.

- `docs/BELLAGIO-PLAN.md`: reference sources, room coordinates and architectural scope.
- `build/level/vegas.glb`: runtime mesh, registered as `level-vegas` with the project CDN.
- `tools/level/vegas.py`: deterministic authoring script; uses the installed Bitmagic Blender helper library.
- `tools/level/render_vegas.py`: three cinematic renders of the real level (not concept art).
- `src/work/vegas-missions.json`: supply positions, six horde bays, emergence points and dormant population (540).
- `docs/HERO-CHARACTER-BRIEFS.md`: nine character requests and rig/texture/animation delivery requirements.
- `docs/WORLD-PLAN.md`: geography, routes and safe-zone gameplay contract.

Rebuild with Blender 5.2+:
```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python tools/level/vegas.py
```
Then follow the project Blender skill: review renders, run both collision audits, re-register the GLB using the printed native height, run `python3.11 tools/blender-level/sync_world.py build/level/vegas.doors.json build/level/vegas.mesh-level.json src/work/world.json`, copy the mesh-level JSON to `src/work`, check and verify. System Python 3.9 is too old for the supplied sync tool. CLI commands should carry the original creator prompt as instructed in AGENTS.md.

## Bellagio milestone
The first hotel rebuilt beyond massing: curved articulated wings, arched crown windows, rounded central tower with open cupola, tiled lakefront pavilions, shaped fountain lake and south portico. The casino and lobby are real ground-floor rooms, with coffered ceilings, columns, glass ceiling flowers, chandeliers, 40 detailed slot cabinets and five gaming tables. The service corridor connects back to the casino and opens into a staff lounge, storeroom, medical room and security office. Eight automatic doors are synchronized to the engine. Upper hotel floors are exterior-only.

The provisions cache is now in the Bellagio storeroom. Approach around the south side of the lake, enter the columned portico, and take the lobby's west service door. The north gallery provides a second public exit. The adjacent lounge is reserved as the food mission's hidden horde staging room; the characters are still pending.

## Remaining work
This is an initial environment, not a finished UE5-fidelity game. Architecture is recognizable but compressed and simplified, not survey-accurate. The main visual gaps are higher-frequency authored damage, the other hotels’ frontage/interiors and more varied street debris/props. Hero/WebHuman assets are awaiting the creator. No active zombies are spawned. The sanctuary exclusion/perception helper is tested but must be connected to every hostile AI and movement path during that integration. Food and medicine currently act as mission cargo; hunger, injury, weapons, ammunition consumption, enemy behavior and death states are not implemented. Public publishing has not been requested or performed.

## Cover lighting
The playable scene uses a cover-referenced sunset sky, 11-degree amber sun, warm distance haze, reduced sky/ambient fill and a restrained warm contrast grade. `src/work/world.json` holds the lighting/fog/grade; `Game.ts` decodes the generated WebP in sRGB and aligns the sky. The Blender review images above predate this live-game lighting update.

## Facade orientation repair
The export pipeline now recalculates outward normals for all closed authored solids before saving Blender and GLB files. Open decorative sheets retain their authored facing. `tools/level/audit_normals.py` checks signed volumes, street-side facade ray normals and single-sided GLB facade materials. Run with Blender and `-- build/facade-repair/normals-after.json --require-clean`. The repair corrected 2,463 reversed shells; 21,584 closed solids now pass, including the curved facade, columns and arch stones. Physics geometry is unchanged.

The repaired CDN export passed a close-up WebGPU game review (`build/facade-repair/in-game-close.png`), with opaque exterior walls and correctly facing architectural details. Both collision audits still pass with zero failures.

## Paris Las Vegas and Eiffel observation deck
Paris replaces the former box hotel and simple tower with an articulated Bordeaux-style high-rise, blue mansard roofs, dormer windows, French boulevard frontage, the blue/gold balloon, and a four-leg Eiffel lattice tower. The casino has a west entrance from the Strip, a north exit, roulette tables, slot banks, chandeliers and steel tower legs passing through it. The hotel rooms and lower tower galleries are exterior scenery in this pass.

Enter the Paris casino from the west, walk into the central cabin marked **OBSERVATION / 140 M**, and press **L** (touch: **LIFT**). A short fade takes you to the enclosed observation deck; use the same cabin to return. The deck has a complete walking circuit, collision along the safety cage, a canopy and viewing telescopes. `tests/paris-lift.test.mjs` exercises the two-way transition, proximity, fresh-press requirement, held-key protection, vehicle exclusion and cleanup. It is a logic test; browser travel is checked separately.

Geometry lives in `tools/level/paris.py`, invoked by the district builder. `tools/level/render_paris.py` produces exterior, street, casino and deck views. Room envelopes and route nodes are separate: a route node is not treated as a tiny sealed room; the full casino and observation deck are audited as enclosed spaces. Architectural reference: [Caesars Paris property overview](https://newsroom.caesars.com/Property-Fact-Sheets/Paris-Las-Vegas/) and [Eiffel Tower steel contractor](https://www.schuff.com/project/eiffel-tower-at-paris-las-vegas/). This is a gameplay-scaled adaptation rather than a surveyed reconstruction.

Paris validation: 532 colliders, ten doors, 75 volumes and 66 route links. Open audit: 71,841 floor rays, 456 doorway rays and 30,330 route rays, zero failures. Closed audit: 71,841 floor rays and 76,340 envelope rays, zero failures. All 35,348 closed authored meshes face outward; facade probes and single-sided GLB materials pass. The 151 coplanar warnings include structural joins and overlapping hotel masses. The sanctuary opening view now faces Paris.

Runtime checks: desktop WebGPU loaded the new asset with 559 physics bodies, settled at the normal sanctuary spawn and separately at (92,140,-39), and passed. The in-app game confirmed L-operated descent to the casino and ascent back to the observation deck. The full casino/deck walking circuit is covered by the geometry audit; it has not been manually walked end-to-end.
