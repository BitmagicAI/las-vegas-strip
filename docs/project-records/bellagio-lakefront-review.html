VEGAS: AFTERLIGHT / 5 OCTOBER 2026

Bellagio north lakefront

The lakefront is now rebuilt as a waterfront village rather than a decorated flat podium. This is installed geometry, with a mapped lake basin, separate pavilions and rooflines, four rotundas, a glazed arcade, stone balconies, shoreline planting and a scalloped public promenade.

A substantial geometry correction, with further architectural refinement open. Roof heights and facade sections are fitted estimates. The reference is not yet matched at photographic detail. No Google Earth or JERDE photograph is used as an in-game texture.

References and decisions

The JERDE project photograph and rotunda drawing informed the glazed roof, cornices and waterfront composition. The basin uses OpenStreetMap way 22971030. The lake ground surface is removed, so it cannot overlap the water. The sanctuary and its jeep/charger were relocated from the lake to dry ground nearby.

Geometry ownership and static clearance: 3,993 Bellagio route samples and 3,861 map-wide bridge samples pass. Door operation and normal walking were checked in the live game. Typecheck and local WebGPU startup verification pass. Performance is still open: startup reported 24 fps while a second live walkthrough was running. The warmed promenade sample was about 37 fps (27.3ms median; 31.2ms p95). These are local observations, not a controlled before/after comparison.

Still open
