## Decisions for the owner

Retain mapped tower/podium footprints and existing parent hotel interiors. Existing 128 m tower, 17 m podium and 123–128 m crown are inherited modelling estimates, not surveyed dimensions. A crown correction can use a modest inward slope within the existing footprint, original dormer frames and simplified roof fittings; exact wing-height differences remain an explicit discrepancy until the parent interior/collision heights can be reconciled. Pool furnishings must leave verified walks and pool edges clear. Do not invent a public lift to the roof or claim continuous public access from reference labels alone. Correcting the north canopy must be preceded by an installed baseline and a west/south entrance layout check; the monument is not the entrance canopy.

No geometry has been changed or installed by this research checkpoint. All prior remaining items stay open pending implementation and evidence.
