# Paris Arc soffit reference survey

Accessed 2026-11-07. Photographs were viewed in the browser; no third-party media is stored in the repository.

## Official reference

[Caesars Paris press kit](https://newsroom.caesars.com/Property-Fact-Sheets/Paris-Las-Vegas/) links [Exterior – Main Valet](https://s202.q4cdn.com/508919455/files/images/fact-sheets-images/ParisLasVegas/Exterior-2.jpg). The full photograph was visually inspected; acquisition date is unstated. It shows rectangular recessed coffers around and along the Arc barrel vault, molded arch rings, a keystone, relief spandrels and pier panels, upper cornice dentils and framed upper ornaments. The green iron and glass canopy is adjacent, supported by cream columns. The image does not establish present tenant names.

## Bounded decision and inferred geometry

Select the main Arc vault and its two mapped piers (way114697074, way114697072, way114697073) as a bounded three-feature group. Add original shallow coffer frames to the existing plain intrados only, after reviewing the installed baseline. Retain all existing ornament, original main opening, collision, interiors, roads, canopy and other frontage components. Existing source dimensions are radius 4.6 m, spring height 12.5 m and depth 13.2 m; they are inherited modeling estimates, not new measured facts. A tentative nine circumferential by five depth bay pattern and 0.05–0.15 m relief are artistic approximations subject to baseline fit, not surveyed counts. Do not claim whole-Arc or whole-resort completion.
