# Sphere / Venetian employee garages: facade research

Inspected Google Maps aerials and Street View on 2026-11-07. Reference links only; no third-party imagery is copied into assets or the repository. Geometry remains original. Copyright dates do not establish aerial acquisition dates.

- [West facade, January 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=1jRnypp5ULlTDc0R6B6YCw&heading=90&pitch=8): 3791 S Koval Ln; monorail obscures part of facade. Pale framing, warm opaque staff-parking panels.
- [West facade, February 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=qAT5QHzfOMHRwUMLGu_ntw&heading=90&pitch=15): 3736 S Koval Ln; white framing, warm inset spandrel strips, dark open slots with thin vertical metal guard subdivisions. Roof guard and staff-parking panel visible.

Retain existing mapped footprint, 25 m estimate and seven deck intervals. New trim dimensions will be visual estimates, not surveyed measurements. No evidence sufficient to revise private ramp or stair layout.

## Adjacent service building

- [West facade, January 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=nFhIzQgytSo0hMxpSAmhUw&heading=90&pitch=5): UI address 3899 S Koval Ln; facade carries number 3840. - [Aerial](https://www.google.com/maps/@36.1197,-115.1672,347m/data=!3m1!1e3): east-west stepped footprint, central dark rooftop solar strip, roof parking and eastern rooftop structures. Acquisition date unknown; no exact panel count established.

- The article's [photograph](https://www.sands.com/content/uploads/2021/07/solar-energy-powers-properties-in-singapore-and-las-vegas.jpg) was personally inspected: it depicts Marina Bay Sands, Singapore. **Rejected as Las Vegas geometry evidence.** Official guest-parking pages concern different garages and do not substantiate this employee structure.

Retain existing 42 m estimate, fifteen deck surfaces and footprint. Do not infer private floor connections or a measured floor count from partially obstructed photographs.

## Decisions for the owner

Reversible facade-only scope across three mapped buildings: refine Wynn garage spandrels/guard framing; replace service-building slat appearance with reference-supported bronze glazing and sparse mullions; add Venetian north facade projecting brown frames. Preserve existing ramps, decks, roads and collision. Solar layout remains approximate; historical panel count does not justify inventing a new layout. No new public interiors are supported by this research. This is a refinement pass, not whole-block or photorealistic completion.
